Strategic Foundation

Generating consistent organic reach by focusing on approachable, high-converting video formats.

Brand Positioning

Persona: Polished, bold, and aspirational ("bougie" yet approachable luxury).

Value Proposition: Perfect casual and lifestyle bespoke fitting, high-quality African craftsmanship, versatile styling, and seamless worldwide delivery.

Tone of Voice: Sophisticated, confident, helpful, and culturally relevant.

Audience Profile

Primary Demographics: Men and women aged 18–45 seeking premium everyday bespoke wear.

Psychographics: Fashion-forward professionals, entrepreneurs, and trendsetters who value exclusivity, impeccable fit, and personalized lifestyle attire.

8 Strategic Video Pillars

Structured formats designed for scale, engagement, and conversion.

01

Get Ready With Me (GRWM)

Fast-paced, aesthetic styling transition from base layers to fully accessorized bespoke outfits.

  • Casual Business Lunch
  • Sunday Brunch / Weekend
  • Travel Two-Piece Styling
02

1 Outfit, X Ways

Demonstrating how a single bespoke garment spans multiple settings & dress codes.

  • Styled 3 Ways: Errands, Coffee, Drinks
  • Day & Night Transitions
  • Sneakers vs. Heels/Slides
03

Real-World Lifestyle

Candid outdoor clips capturing casual wear in premium everyday settings.

  • Grocery run but make it bespoke
  • Coffee pickup in casual chic
  • Mall stroll fit-checks
04

Shortlet & Multi-Set

Leveraging luxury locations to shoot varied high-end looks in single sessions.

  • Poolside / Resort Wear
  • Balcony Golden Hour
  • Lounge Structured Separates
05

Craftsmanship & BTS

Highlighting the bespoke journey from fabric selection to final hand-stitching.

  • Virtual measurements (Global)
  • Fabric inspection & cutting
  • Detail work & hand-embroidery
06

Client Spotlights

Client reactions, shipment packaging, and featured customer submissions.

  • Genuine fitting reactions
  • Global shipping routes map overlay
  • Sketch vs. Final comparisons
07

Trend Transitions

Fast 5–10 second viral transition videos matched with algorithm-trending audio.

  • Beat-synced cuts
  • Snap-of-a-finger switches
  • Camera spin transitions
08

Color Theory & Styling

Educational breakdowns of palettes, tonal combos, and accessory pairing.

  • Monochrome vs Complementary
  • Accessory Matching
  • Elevating Minimalist Outfits

Production & Growth Tactics

The operational backend to sustain 21+ pieces of weekly content.

Batching Workflow

Dedicated Filming (1x Weekly): One full day or two half-days to shoot 10–12 looks.

Lighting & Audio: Natural daylight / softbox. Wireless lapel mic for clear voiceovers.

Camera: 4K at 60fps, downsampled for TikTok/Reels.

Templates: Pre-written hooks ("Stop scrolling if...") and standardized CTAs for international shipping.

Growth & Conversion

Influencers: Monthly micro/macro collaborations wearing bespoke lifestyle outfits.

Paid Social: Boost high-performing organic GRWM and styling videos globally.

Automated Funnel: ManyChat automated DM responses for "FIT" or "BESPOKE" keywords.

Linktree: Direct funnels to [Book Fitting], [WhatsApp], and [Order Guides].
